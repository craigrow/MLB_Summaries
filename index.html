In a pivotal Game 2, the Rays capitalized on a five-run fifth inning to defeat the Yankees 5-2, extending their series lead to 2-0 in the ALDS. Junior Caminero's early force out gave Tampa Bay a lead they would not relinquish, as they combined aggressive base running with timely hitting to keep the Yankees on their heels.

Despite Ben Rice's impressive two-homer performance for New York, including a shot in the fourth that briefly knotted the game, the Yankees' pitching faltered. Cam Schlittler struggled through 4.1 innings, allowing four runs, while Rays starter Freddy Peralta and reliever Ian Seymour held the Yankees to just six hits between them. The sellout crowd at Tropicana Field celebrated loudly as Bryan Baker closed out the game, setting the stage for a potential sweep in Game 3.

The White Sox edged the Guardians 4-3 in a nail-biter at Progressive Field, taking a commanding 2-0 lead in the ALDS. Sean Burke dazzled on the mound, striking out six batters over 5.1 innings while allowing just one hit, but it was a costly throwing error by White Sox pitcher Sean Newcomb in the first inning that put the Guardians ahead early.

Braden Montgomery's two-run double in the sixth turned the tide for the White Sox, who rallied to take the lead. Chase Meidroth added an insurance run shortly after, giving Chicago a 4-2 advantage. Jo Adell's eighth-inning triple kept the Guardians within striking distance, but Grant Taylor closed the door, securing the win and leaving the Guardians in a precarious position as the series shifts to Chicago.
