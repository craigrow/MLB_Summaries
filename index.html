Brice Turang's two-run homer in the fifth inning propelled the Brewers to a 3-2 victory over the Cardinals on Saturday, pushing Milwaukee to the brink of a series sweep. The near sellout crowd of 42,900 erupted as Turang sent a blast to center, turning a slim deficit into a lead that the Brewers would not relinquish.

Quinn Mathews struck out seven over five solid innings for St. Louis, but it was not enough to overcome Kyle Harrison's strong performance on the mound for Milwaukee. The Brewers' rookie hurler allowed three runs on five hits and fanned five before handing the ball to Aaron Ashby, who secured the save. Alec Burleson kept the Cardinals in the mix with a solo homer, but it was Turang's decisive swing that ultimately sealed the night for the home team.
