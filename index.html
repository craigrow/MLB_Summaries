The Brewers continued their postseason surge, taking a commanding 3-0 lead in the National League Championship Series with a 6-2 victory over the Dodgers in front of a raucous crowd of 50,708 on Wednesday night. Jackson Chourio opened the scoring with a first-inning homer, and after the Dodgers briefly took the lead, Milwaukee's offense answered back with a flurry of late-inning fireworks.

Jake Bauers and Garrett Mitchell each blasted pivotal homers in the seventh, propelling the Brewers past Blake Snell, who struck out nine but was unable to contain the late onslaught. Robert Gasser delivered a stellar performance from the mound, tossing five scoreless innings to keep the Dodgers off balance. Trevor Megill sealed the win, allowing the Brewers to inch closer to a World Series berth.
