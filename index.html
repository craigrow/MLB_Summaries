In a strong start to their four-game series against the Astros, the Mariners secured a 3-1 victory on Monday night, backed by a stellar performance from George Kirby. The young right-hander struck out seven over five innings, allowing just one run despite being touched for seven hits. Julio Rodríguez provided the fireworks, blasting his seventh homer of the season and helping the Mariners build an early lead that they would never relinquish.

Dominic Canzone and Cole Young contributed with key singles that put Seattle ahead 2-0 in the second, paving the way for Rodríguez's solo shot shortly after. The Astros managed to cut the deficit with a run in the fifth, but the Mariners' bullpen held strong, with Andrés Muñoz earning the save. With this win, Seattle fans can feel optimistic as their team aims to build momentum in a highly competitive AL West.
