Cooper Pratt drove in two runs, including a crucial insurance run on a sacrifice fly in the sixth, while David Hamilton added another RBI single. The Rays managed to break through in the ninth with a double from Chandler Simpson, but it wasn’t enough to overcome Milwaukee’s early lead. As the crowd of 40,489 looked on, the Brewers' pitching and timely hitting set the stage for a potential series clincher in the next game.
