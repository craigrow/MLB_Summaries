The White Sox stormed into Houston and seized control of the ALDS with a 6-3 victory over the Astros on Tuesday night, led by a stellar performance from Jordan Hicks and timely hitting from their lineup.

A sacrifice fly from Munetaka Murakami put Chicago on the board early, but it was the bat of Colson Montgomery that electrified the crowd, as he crushed a home run to extend the lead to 4-0. While Nelson Velázquez briefly sparked a rally for the Astros with a two-run shot, the White Sox held firm. Chase Meidroth's two-hit night and a wild pitch that allowed Braden Montgomery to reach first kept the pressure on Houston, as the White Sox secured a crucial opening win in the best-of-three series.

The Yankees left no doubt as they began their quest for a championship, leaving the Red Sox reeling ahead of Game 2.

Michael King was untouchable on the mound, tossing seven shutout innings while striking out eight and allowing just one hit. Austin Riley launched a pivotal eighth-inning homer, propelling the Braves to a 5-3 victory over the Phillies in Game 1 of their postseason series on Tuesday. Both teams exchanged blows throughout the evening, with the Phillies initially taking a 1-0 lead thanks to Alec Bohm's first-inning double. J.T. Realmuto later tripled in the seventh to briefly give Philadelphia a 3-2 advantage, but the Braves answered back emphatically. Dylan Lee earned the win for Atlanta, while Raisel Iglesias secured the save, showcasing the depth of the Braves’ bullpen as they look to gain momentum in the playoffs. Jhoan Duran took the loss for the Phillies.
