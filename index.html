Jo Adell powered the Guardians to a crucial 9-3 victory over the White Sox on Wednesday, staying alive in the AL Division Series and cutting Chicago's series lead to 2-1. Adell’s three RBIs, including a pivotal three-run triple in the third inning, set the tone as Cleveland's offense erupted against a team that had dominated them in the first two games.

Foster Griffin earned the win with a solid performance, allowing just two runs over six innings. The nearly sold-out crowd of 40,590 at Guaranteed Rate Field witnessed the Guardians' resurgence, leaving the series still very much alive.

The Brewers took a commanding 3-1 lead in the National League Division Series, fueled by a stellar pitching performance from Aaron Ashby and a clutch hitting display. Ashby tossed three hitless innings in relief of Robert Gasser, who worked four strong frames, allowing only one run. With the crowd of 47,712 on edge, Garrett Mitchell’s sharp single in the third gave Milwaukee an early edge, before Jackson Chourio extended the lead in the fifth.

Despite a solo shot from Fernando Tatis Jr. that briefly tied the game, the Brewers answered back with two more runs, including a sacrifice fly from Sal Frelick that brought in Jake Bauers. Trevor Megill shut the door in the ninth, securing the victory and putting Milwaukee one win away from advancing to the next round. With the Padres on the brink of elimination, the Brewers are primed to seal the series in Game 5.
