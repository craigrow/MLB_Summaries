In a tense postseason showdown, Drew Rasmussen silenced the Yankees’ bats with a brilliant eight-inning performance, leading the Rays to a 1-0 victory in the opening game of their five-game series. Gerrit Cole matched Rasmussen's intensity through five innings, allowing just one run while striking out five, but the Yankees couldn't muster any offense against the stellar Rays right-hander. With a near sellout crowd of 40,542 roaring them on, Tampa Bay's bullpen sealed the win as Bryan Baker notched the save, putting the Yankees in an early hole in the series.
