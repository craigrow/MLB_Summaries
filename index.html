In a wild slugfest at Nationals Park, the Braves took advantage of a shaky outing from Zack Littell, outlasting the Nationals 8-6 to seize a 2-1 series lead. Michael Harris II starred with two homers and three RBIs, helping the Braves rally from an early 4-1 deficit. Drake Baldwin's first-inning blast hinted at what was to come, but it was Harris's second home run that put Atlanta ahead for good.

Despite a spirited fight from Daylen Lile, who went deep and drove in three runs for the Nationals, the Braves' offense proved too potent. Matt Olson added a three-run homer in the fourth, extending the lead to 8-4, as Atlanta’s pitching staff navigated the late-game threats from Washington. Dylan Lee picked up the win in a game marked by inconsistent pitching, while Robert Suarez notched a save amid the sparse crowd of 16,308.
