In a disappointing start to the series, the Mariners fell 11-4 to the Twins on Monday, as Luis Castillo struggled to find his rhythm against a potent Minnesota lineup. Castillo allowed seven runs over five innings, surrendering multiple homers—including a two-run shot by Kody Clemens, who drove in five runs on the night. The sparse crowd of 14,160 at Target Field didn’t have to wait long to see the Twins jump out to a lead, as they piled on the runs early, leaving Seattle in a deep hole.

The Mariners managed a modest response, highlighted by Cal Raleigh's seventh homer of the season, which brought home Rob Refsnyder in the eighth inning. However, it wasn't nearly enough to overcome the damage done by the Twins' offense, which was relentless throughout the game. Mitch Garver and Cole Young added runs with timely hits, but the Mariners couldn’t capitalize on their opportunities against Twins' starter Connor Prielipp, who struck out five in his five innings of work. With two more games in the series, Seattle will need to regroup quickly if they hope to turn the tide.
