The Braves powered their way to a commanding 6-2 win over the Phillies in the decisive Game 3, securing a 2-1 series lead in the National League Division Series. Michael Harris II ignited the offense with a three-run homer in the first inning, followed by Ozzie Albies and Matt Olson adding their own long balls to keep the pressure on Philadelphia’s pitching staff.

Aaron Nola struggled early, lasting just 1.2 innings before giving way to Zack Wheeler, who provided some relief but couldn’t stem the tide of the Braves’ offense. Grant Holmes delivered a stellar performance from the mound, tossing 5.0 innings and allowing only two runs while fanning six. With this win, Atlanta is one step closer to advancing, while the Phillies will need to regroup as they face elimination in Game 4.
