In a thrilling opener at Camden Yards, Pete Alonso smashed two home runs, including a game-winner in the eighth inning, leading the Orioles to a come-from-behind 4-3 victory over the Blue Jays on Monday night. The sparse crowd of just over 12,000 witnessed the Orioles rally from an early 3-0 deficit, thanks to a stellar performance by Alonso, who drove in three runs and reached base in all four at-bats.

Trey Yesavage started strong for the Blue Jays, striking out three in two innings before the Orioles found their rhythm against the bullpen. Shane Baz delivered a solid outing for Baltimore, allowing three runs over 6.2 innings, paving the way for Yennier Cano to earn the win and Rico Garcia to secure the save. Gunnar Henderson also made his mark, going 3-for-4 with a homer and 1 RBI, as the Orioles showcased their resilience in a crucial stretch of the season.
