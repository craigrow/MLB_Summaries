Yoshinobu Yamamoto delivered a dazzling performance, striking out ten batters over seven innings, as the Dodgers edged the Braves 3-1 in Game 3 of the National League Division Series on Tuesday. With the series knotted at one game apiece, the right-hander stifled Atlanta’s offense, allowing just four hits and one run while keeping the pressure on the Braves' hitters throughout.

Enrique Hernández and Freddie Freeman provided the firepower for Los Angeles, each launching a homer in the fourth inning that put the game out of reach for Chris Sale, who struggled to contain the Dodgers' lineup. Drake Baldwin's groundout in the fifth brought Atlanta closer, but Edwin Díaz slammed the door shut, preserving the lead and putting the Dodgers one game away from advancing in the postseason.
