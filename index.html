In a thrilling postseason clash, the Braves edged the Dodgers 3-2 on Sunday, leveling the National League Division Series at one game apiece. Mauricio Dubón set the tone early with an RBI single in the first, before the Dodgers' Freddie Freeman answered back with a towering homer in the fourth. However, Atlanta’s Michael Harris II found his groove, driving in an important insurance run with a double in the seventh that proved crucial as the Dodgers rallied late.

Despite a late charge featuring Max Muncy's second home run of the playoffs, the Braves’ bullpen held firm, with Dylan Lee earning the win and Raisel Iglesias shutting the door in the ninth. Ray Kerr and Didier Fuentes combined for 6.0 innings, demonstrating the depth of Atlanta's pitching staff. With a near-sellout crowd of 50,729 on hand, the tension will only escalate as the series shifts back to Atlanta for Game 3.
